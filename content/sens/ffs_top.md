# Important things to know

- Get someone to be with you for 4-5 days
- It costed me 159,000 INR for surgery + Bed (2 days non AC)
- Get CT-Scan Dicom files from the operator, it can be mailed or CD
- Don't pack winter wear, Kochi is hot!

# -10 days

Had a consult with Dr Arjun Ashokan from Renai Medicity, spoke with him and discussed what the procedure will be and what timeline and recovery i'll be going through

# -5 days

Booked flights and Hotels in Kochi for 10 days, Had my CT Scan and gave myself more dysphoria on jaw too but eh I dont want to risk anymore.

# -2 days

Flight day

# -1 day - 2nd Sept 2026

Had blood tests and ... anxiety maxxing, went on a small city explore, Fort Kochi and other places.

I was initially told to come alone to get admitted but later they asked for a bystander to be present during admission and yea... was not allowed to leave hospital after that.

Got admitted for the night and paid the advance (100k INR)

Had to order spare clothes and towel since I wasn't given any in the hospital, the AC rooms ran out so I had to pick a non AC room which was pretty alright.

# OP day - 3rd Sept 2026

Was on empty stomach since 10pm yesterday, and no water intake since midnight today. Nurses came to get me changed into the gown and had my nails cut for safety.

*0630h* I was woken up for prepping, asked to take a bath and change, Amara braided my hair and was taken in a wheelchair to the main room.

<img width="530" height="399" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/64d26593-3c45-4831-a80f-9fadc3d3a529" />

*0800h* i was pulled into the pre surgery waiting room where I was hard panic anxiety sitting there

*0830h* i finally met my doctor, they took my pre-op photos for reference and had a briefing on surgery again.

*0900h* was pulled into Operation Theater, I was watching the ceiling and going deeper and deeper into the hospital and eventually went into the OT, There was a clock there which I was looking at.

(Hazy timeline)

I was given Oxygen to breath on, a mask was put on me while they attach all the vital sensors around me. I was hearing my heartbeat as beeps, had BP and other machines.

There was an overhead light on me shining extremely bright lol, and being poked on the side for IV fluid.

Nurses were moving around and stuff and it was just surreal.

*0915h* is the last time I saw in the clock before I finally dozed off, this was weird it was not like I was sleeping it just was very weird.

I dont remember dozing off just the entire time just got chopped out of my brain as if that never happened, its not like falling asleep at all.

*1400h* Finally woke up? first thing I saw was 02:00 and I was freaking out thinking it was 2 AM.. first thing I asked the person beside me was its 2 AM? and she said no its 2 PM. Then she asked me my name and what I remember. This felt like very TV series-y.

I felt no pain, a little tightness on my forehead and I did gently touch it ... omg EUPHORIA!

Had a Drain attached to me and um it was just sucking up blood, there was nothing much in it but yeah it was attached to me and its weird.

*1500h-1700h* I was kept in observation, mouth very dry and dehydrated, had feeling of throwing up so asked bed to be raised a little bit and asked for a mug, but nothing coming out. Nurses were very helpful and met someone who was talking to me in Telugu.

*1800h* finally was cleared to drink water ... omg water is heaven!!! went back to my room, saw missed calls from parents who I havent told at all about surgery so was freaking out. My brother covered for me!

*1900h+* I was just sleeping, too tired to look at phone but I was listening to podcast and talking to Amara.

All night Nurses were changing my IV painkiller, I still haven't felt any pain which I told her and she said doctor will meet me tomorrow.

*1100h* Doctor came and checked up on me and said drain can be removed and they removed my IV too, Doctor said I can explore the city, and the surgery came out really well, and very minimal swelling
