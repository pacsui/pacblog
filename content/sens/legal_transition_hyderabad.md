## Documents Updating

### Aadhar
> Done - 3 hours

Changing Gender on Aadhar doesn't require any documents.

[Updating Gender on Aadhar - AfterTheSilence](https://afterthesilence.org/blogs/how-to-update-name-and-gender-on-aadhaar-card)

> To update your gender, you only need to self-declare the gender you identify with. No medical proof, gazette notification, or transgender ID card is required. You can read about UIDAI's official guidance on handling name and gender changes in Aadhaar cards here.

#### First Appointment - 21st March 2026

Appointment scheduled in **Ameerpet ASK (Aadhar Seva Kendra)**, **Donot go here!** 

People are not helpful and are not ready to listen to official guidelines, I was ridiculed and was told that changing gender will cause issues for me in future.

#### Second Appointment - 17th Sept 2026

Appointment in **Raidurg ASK (Aadhar Seva Kendra)**

Went in with my Appointment number to Counter A and got my Token.

Counter B they took my biometrics, updated my gender.

UIDAI Portal and Aadhar app data were updated in 3 hours, later resynced on digilocker.

### PAN [ ⌼ ⌽ ]
> Done - 11 days

Updating PAN is straight forward process, log into your PAN portal and update gender.
Note that documents have to be scanned under 2MB.

Took me 4 days for verification confirmation and Physical PAN delivered in +1 week from verification.

[Updating Gender on PAN - AfterTheSilence](https://afterthesilence.org/blogs/how-to-update-your-name-and-gender-on-your-pan-card)

### Appointment - 3rd Nov 2026

Appointment in Begumpet PSK at 9:15 AM, Reached there at 8:45.

#### Token Counter

**Documents Needed**

- Aadhar - ORG
- PAN - ORG
- Previous passport - Photocopy

_Got token N-23_

#### Counter A 

Got my photo taken, They asked I should check my passport next time for mistyped gender marker. Told them I am Trans woman and showed them my TG Card Section 7.

They scanned uploaded:

- Aadhar 
- PAN
- Passport 

They said TG Card is not required to be scanned and to come back if Counter C requests for it.

#### Counter B

They validated my Aadhar, PAN, asked why I am applying new passport when its under validity, mentioned its demographic update.

#### Counter C

Met the Granting officer, He asked the similar question on why I am applying for new passport when its valid. I told its about Gender marker being M and I want it as F.

He told me I should be carefully check during passport process if its typed incorrectly, Told him I'm Trans woman, he said okay.

He asked couple of questions on where I work, Why my name has a `D` without any expansion for which I showed my Birth Certificate ( Which is from Maharashtra ). Got asked couple of questions on where my schooling and University education were done, Why I moved from Maharashtra to Hyderabad (I told him how we moved to Mumbai Pune Machilipatnam due to dad being in government).

He did suggest me to change my name by expanding `D` to reduce further complications in future.

He punched my passport and said its granted, well I did ask after that if TG Card is needed to be scanned, or any other documents? He said not needed.

### Police Verification - 6th Nov 2026

Standard documents were asked to be checked

- Aadhar
- CBSE
- JNTUH
- Dad's Aadhar 
- Electricity Bill

My Dad chimed in to do signature, where Police asked if I (His Daughter) has any siblings for which he said "He's not my daughter but my son who is changing his gender".

for which he asked me why and how passport office changed the gender. I gave him
